# Wrong Turn

> 🚧 **Still in Development**

**Wrong Turn** is a fast-paced psychological arcade game built around one simple idea:

> **The arrow lies.**

The game challenges players to question their instincts instead of blindly following what they see. Directional cues can be deceptive, forcing players to stay focused, react quickly, and adapt as the rules change.

## 🎮 The Concept

Wrong Turn is designed to test more than just reaction speed.

Players must:

* 👀 Observe the directional cues
* 🧠 Think before reacting
* ⚡ Make quick decisions under pressure
* 🔄 Adapt when the rules change
* 🎯 Maintain focus as the difficulty increases

What looks like the obvious choice may be the **wrong turn**.

## 🧩 Core Gameplay

The game revolves around deceptive directional mechanics.

As the player progresses, the game introduces increasingly challenging situations where:

* Arrows may provide misleading information
* Familiar patterns can suddenly change
* Reaction speed alone isn't enough
* Players must recognize and adapt to changing rules
* Mistakes become increasingly costly

The goal is simple:

**Don't follow the arrow. Follow the rule.**

## 📊 Player Challenge

Wrong Turn focuses on four core abilities:

| Skill              | What It Tests                                |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| ⚡ Reflex           | How quickly you can respond                  |
| 🧠 Focus           | How well you maintain concentration          |
| 🔄 Adaptability    | How quickly you adjust to new rules          |
| 🎯 Decision Making | Whether you can resist instinctive reactions |
