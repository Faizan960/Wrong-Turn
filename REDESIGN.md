## 1 · Design Pillars

1. **The arrow IS the game.** One giant glossy blue arrow on near-black. Everything else whispers.
2. **Answering feels physical.** Every input lands inside 3 frames with scale, light, sound.
3. **Failure is loud, retry is instant.** Death slams; the retry button is under the thumb before the shame fades.
4. **The next carrot is always visible.** Best score, next milestone, next unlock — one glance away, never modal.
